# aXantiq Brand Voice Guide

> **The one rule that never bends: it's always aXantiq. The X is always capitalized. Always.**

---

## 1. Brand Personality

If aXantiq were a person, they'd be the senior engineer at the party who's already three steps ahead of the conversation — but makes you feel like you're keeping up. They don't explain what AI is. They assume you're smart enough to be here. They build things that work, speak plainly about what doesn't, and occasionally drop a movie reference just to see who's paying attention.

aXantiq is the friend who answers "Can you help me with my IT?" with "I already started."

---

## 2. Voice Attributes

### Confident, not arrogant

- **We are**: Direct, assured, and backed by experience. We've done this before.
- **We are not**: Bragging, name-dropping, or talking down to anyone.
- **This sounds like**: "We build end-to-end AI solutions. That's the whole pitch."
- **This does NOT sound like**: "We are the leading revolutionary next-gen paradigm-shifting AI powerhouse."

### Sharp, not sarcastic

- **We are**: Witty, clever, occasionally provocative. We like a well-placed wink.
- **We are not**: Mean-spirited, mocking, or trying too hard to be funny.
- **This sounds like**: "You know what we do. That's why you're here. ;-)"
- **This does NOT sound like**: "LOL you really thought you could do this without us?"

### Technical, not jargon-heavy

- **We are**: Precise and knowledgeable. We speak the language of builders.
- **We are not**: Hiding behind buzzwords or overcomplicating things to sound smart.
- **This sounds like**: "We implemented a RAG pipeline that cut response latency by 60%."
- **This does NOT sound like**: "Our synergistic AI-driven ecosystem leverages cutting-edge paradigms."

### Bold, not reckless

- **We are**: Willing to challenge conventions, take strong positions, and say what others won't.
- **We are not**: Controversial for the sake of attention, or dismissive of proven approaches.
- **This sounds like**: "Most AI strategies fail because they start with the technology, not the problem."
- **This does NOT sound like**: "Every other IT company is garbage."

## 3. Audience Awareness

**Primary audience**: Decision-makers at companies who know they need AI but don't want to get burned by hype. CTOs, founders, operations leads — people who've heard enough pitches and want someone who just delivers.

**Secondary audience**: Technical teams who will work alongside aXantiq. Developers and engineers who need to trust that we know what we're doing.

**What they care about**: Results. Speed to implementation. Not having to explain basics. A partner who gets it without a 40-slide deck.

**How they expect to be addressed**: As equals. No hand-holding, no condescension, no "let me explain what AI means."

---

## 4. Core Messaging Pillars

**Pillar 1 — We build, not just advise.**
aXantiq isn't a slide deck shop. We consult, yes — but we also implement, integrate, and ship. End-to-end means end-to-end.

**Pillar 2 — AI-native, everything.**
Every solution we touch has AI woven in. It's not a bolt-on feature; it's the foundation. This is what we do, and we do it across the full stack of IT.

**Pillar 3 — If you're here, you already know.**
We don't waste time convincing people that AI matters. Our clients come to us because they've already decided. We respect that.

**Pillar 4 — Systems that actually work.**
We don't build demos. We build production systems, work systems, e2e solutions — things that run when we're not in the room.

## 5. Tone Spectrum

The voice stays the same. The dial moves between **bold/provocative** and **technical/precise** depending on context.

| Context                               | Tone                                         | Example                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Landing page / first impression       | Bold, confident, a touch of humor            | "You know what we do. That's why you're here."                                  |
| Proposal / client pitch               | Precise, direct, results-focused             | "Here's the architecture. Here's the timeline. Here's what changes."            |
| Technical documentation               | Clean, no-nonsense, respectful of the reader | "This endpoint accepts a JSON payload with the following schema."               |
| Social media                          | Punchy, opinionated, occasionally playful    | "Hot take: your AI strategy doesn't need more data. It needs better questions." |
| Error / incident                      | Transparent, accountable, brief              | "Something broke. We're on it. ETA: 2 hours."                                   |
| Easter eggs (like the swordfish page) | Clever, cinematic, reward the curious        | "Password? You should know this one."                                           |

## 6. Style Rules

### The Name

- Always written as **aXantiq** — lowercase 'a', uppercase 'X', rest lowercase.
- Never: Axantiq, AXANTIQ, axantiq, AxantiQ, or any other variation.
- In code, filenames, or URLs where mixed case isn't possible, use `axantiq` (all lowercase) as the fallback.

### Grammar & Mechanics

| Rule                       | Choice                                        |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Contractions               | Yes — we're conversational                    |
| Oxford comma               | Yes                                           |
| Sentence case for headings | Yes                                           |
| Numbers                    | Numerals for all (even 1-9) — we're technical |
| Em dashes                  | No spaces — like this                         |
| Exclamation marks          | Sparingly. One per page max. Earn it.         |

### Formatting

- Keep copy short. If it takes a paragraph, it should probably take a sentence.
- Use monospace (`Geist Mono`) for anything that feels like code, commands, or technical labels.
- White space is a feature, not a bug.

### Language to Avoid

| Avoid                  | Why                     | Use instead                           |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| "Cutting-edge"         | Overused, means nothing | Be specific about what's new          |
| "Leverage" (as a verb) | Corporate jargon        | "Use"                                 |
| "Solution" (alone)     | Vague                   | Say what it actually does             |
| "Empower"              | Patronizing             | "Enable" or just describe the outcome |
| "Disrupt"              | Tired                   | "Change" or describe the change       |
| "Synergy"              | Never                   | Just... never                         |

### Language to Embrace

- "Build" — we make things
- "Ship" — we deliver
- "Works" — the highest compliment
- Movie and pop-culture references (when they land naturally)
- Direct address: "you", "your", "we"

| aXantiq        | Axantiq, axantiq, AXANTIQ                             |
| AI solutions   | AI-powered solutions                                  |
| End-to-end     | E2E (in client-facing copy)                           |
| Implementation | Deployment (unless literally deploying code)          |
| Work systems   | Workflows (when referring to our full-stack approach) |

### The Swordfish Rule

aXantiq embraces Easter eggs and cultural references. The "swordfish" password page is the archetype: a gate that rewards the initiated and sets the tone before a single word of copy is read. When in doubt about whether something is "too much" — if it would make a smart person smile, it stays.

## 8. Visual Voice (Cross-reference with Brand Assets)

The visual identity reinforces the verbal voice:

- **Neon green (#00ff88) on near-black (#0a0a0a)**: Terminal aesthetic. We live in the code.
- **Geist Mono**: Monospace = precision. No serifs, no fluff.
- **The "aX" icon**: The brand distilled to 2 characters. Lowercase meets uppercase. Unassuming meets bold.
- **The X treatment**: Regular weight at 110% size — same stroke weight, just bigger. It stands out through scale, not thickness.

Everything should feel like it was built by someone who ships production code — because it was.
